# Elements

Every element this toolkit holds or plans to, by the name to use when asking for it in another project ("add the Notice card from NiceToHave"). One row each, alphabetical. A new element gets a row the day it gets an idea note, and its status moves as it does.

**Status**: `Idea` (asked for, no rules yet), `Rules written` (a Research note says how it works), `In the repo` (code in its folder, ready to drop in).

| Element | What it is | Status | Learned from | Rules |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Add to Home Screen tip | A Notice card that shows a phone the steps to add the app to its Home Screen, on at most three days, never once installed | Rules written | Astrevelar | [[Notice card and the Add to Home Screen tip]] |
| Feedback to Telegram | An in-app feedback and bug report flow that sends each report to a Telegram chat | Idea | Speaker Tape | [[A feedback and bug report flow that lands in Telegram]] |
| Notice card | A small closable card at the top of a page, saying one thing once; the X keeps it away | Rules written | Astrevelar (Tarot Studio) | [[Notice card and the Add to Home Screen tip]] |
| Walkthrough | A first-visit tour: cards that ring each part of a dense screen in turn, shown once, replayable from settings | Rules written | Astrevelar (Tarot Studio) | [[Walkthrough]] |

Every element keeps its shape and behaviour and takes the look of the app it lands in.
